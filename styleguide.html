Product Direction

A quiet daily planner with paper-like constraints.

Burner List should feel focused, warm, and low-friction. The interface is an operational tool, but not a corporate dashboard: it should have the calm of paper, the precision of a task app, and just enough charm to make daily prioritization feel humane.

01

Design Principles

Use these as decision filters when Claude Design or future app work proposes changes.

Focused over complete.
The app should help users choose what matters today, not expose every possible productivity feature at once.
Paper calm, app precision.
Keep surfaces restrained and readable, but make interactions crisp, synced, and forgiving.
Dense enough for real work.
Avoid marketing-style hero layouts inside the app. Prioritize scanning, editing, and repeated daily use.
Charm in details.
Use the handwritten title, soft themes, quotes, and subtle motion sparingly. The task list remains the center.

Usage Guidelines

A concise handoff brief for improving the design while preserving product identity.

Do
  • Preserve the paper-like Burner List as the central object.
  • Use restrained, legible UI patterns suitable for repeated daily editing.
  • Strengthen component consistency: buttons, rows, modals, sidebar, menus.
  • Improve mobile ergonomics and iOS standalone app feel.
  • Use icons for compact tools and text for clear commands.
Do Not
  • Do not make it look like a marketing landing page.
  • Do not bury tasks inside decorative cards or heavy chrome.
  • Do not overuse gradients, oversized type, or playful illustrations.
  • Do not remove the constraint-driven Front/Back/Kitchen Sink hierarchy.
  • Do not introduce a one-note color palette that loses the theme system.
